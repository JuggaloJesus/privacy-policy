Privacy Policy — Hyper Kasual

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy covers the Android apps published by Hyper Kasual:

  • Games: Neon Breaker, Boom Bricks, Floppy Turd, Grand Theft Turd, Helix Drop, Neon Dodge, Fruit Splat, Stack Up, ZigZag Rush, Gravity Flip, Knife Khaos, WICKED
  • Wavelength (music hosting app)
  • BeatForge (beat-making app)
  • MicDrop (multitrack audio recording app)

The short version

Our apps do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Everything you do in our apps stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Our apps contain no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, and no third-party data collection of any kind. The games do not require an internet connection and do not transmit any data.

What stays on your device

  • Games: high scores, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device only (in the app's private storage). They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Wavelength: if you create a Listener or Creator account, your username and password are stored only on your device — passwords are stored as irreversible SHA-256 hashes, never as plain text. Audio you upload stays in the app's private storage on your device. There are no servers and no data leaves your phone.
  • BeatForge: every beat you create stays on your device. Beat codes are plain text you can copy; they are only shared if you choose to share them yourself.
  • MicDrop: everything you record stays on your device. Audio is captured only when you tap record, and WAV mixdowns are written to your Downloads/MicDrop folder only when you choose to export. Nothing is ever uploaded.

Permissions

  • Our games request no sensitive permissions. They do not access your contacts, location, microphone, camera, or files outside their own private storage.
  • MicDrop requests microphone access, which is required for its core function (recording audio). The microphone is used only while you are actively recording, and the recorded audio never leaves your device.

Children

Our games are family-friendly comedy and arcade titles suitable for general audiences. Since we collect no data from anyone, there is nothing special to disclose for children under 13.
